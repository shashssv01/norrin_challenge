What technical documentation does a high-risk AI system need?

smart_toy Source: AI Lens
High Confidence

Under the EU AI Act, high-risk AI systems must possess technical documentation drawn up before the system is placed on the market or put into service, and it must be kept up to date.

According to gavel Art. 11 and the specific elements detailed in description Annex IV, the documentation must demonstrate compliance with the requirements set out in Title III, Chapter 2. Key requirements include:

  • A general description of the AI system, its intended purpose, and versions of relevant software or hardware.
  • Detailed description of the elements of the AI system and of the process for its development, including system architecture, algorithmic details, and data requirements.
  • Detailed information about the monitoring, functioning and control of the AI system, particularly with regard to human oversight metrics.
  • A description of the appropriateness of the performance metrics for the specific AI system.
