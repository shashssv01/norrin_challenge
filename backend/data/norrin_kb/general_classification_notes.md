# Norrin Internal Guidance: General Classification Notes

## Source
Based on general AI Act guidelines and Recital 47.

## Key Classification Signals

### MINIMAL_RISK
- AI that suggests variations for human workers to choose from, where humans select and edit all final output.
- Pure human-in-loop creative tools.
- Recommendation engines that do not manipulate or exploit vulnerabilities.

### LIMITED_RISK
- Systems that analyze voice recordings to detect emotional states for transparency but don't use it for consequential decisions.
- Article 50(3) transparency obligations.

## Norrin Recommendation
Check Article 5 first, then Annex III. If neither apply, evaluate transparency needs.
