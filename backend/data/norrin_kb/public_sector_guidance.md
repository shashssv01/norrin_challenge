# Norrin Internal Guidance: Public Sector AI Systems

## Source
Based on EC guidance on public sector AI systems and explicit prohibited practices.

## Key Classification Signals for Public Sector

### PROHIBITED practices
- System assigns citizens a social trustworthiness rating based on behavior or history.
- Explicit prohibited practice under Article 5(1)(c).

### Strong HIGH_RISK indicators
- System deployed by a local authority to rank or prioritize access to essential services or benefits.
- Public sector access to essential services is HIGH_RISK even with human review.

## Norrin Recommendation
Any public sector system affecting access to benefits should be flagged as HIGH_RISK. Any system involving social scoring is PROHIBITED.

## Relevant Articles
- Article 5(1)(c)
- Annex III, point 5(a)
